TENSORAMAX LAB

Open research into language models, systems, and humans.

TensoraMax Lab is an open research and development space exploring LLMs, SLMs, agents, intelligent systems, digital knowledge and their effects on humans. We build, test, document and publish experiments openly.

WHY THIS LIBRARY?

What happens when literature becomes a system?

This experiment asks how a literary archive can become spatial, connected and interactive without losing the actual works. The catalogue remains grounded in the English Literature Library's records, sources and curriculum relationships.

Original archive: the production English Literature Library remains the canonical destination. This edition is the experimental layer.
